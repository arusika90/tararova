06What's nearby

Explore the island

Kapovci sits in the centre of Krk, a good base for the whole island. Ask Robert and Edvin for their favourite spots.

Island of Krk

  • Krk town and Malinska

    Both about 10 minutes by car. In Krk town: the cathedral, Frankopan castle, Venetian walls and the harbour.

  • Beaches of Krk

    The nearest beaches are about 5 minutes by car, with coves and bays all around the island.

  • Island villages

    Baška, Vrbnik, Omišalj and Dobrinj, with konobas and viewpoints along the way.

  • Island sights

    Košljun island and its monastery, Biserujka cave, the healing mud at Soline, the Baška tablet and the aquariums in Baška and Krk.

  • Active days

    Over 200 km of cycling routes, walking trails, Wakeboard Cable Krk, diving and snorkelling, windsurfing, water skiing, two zip-line parks, horse riding and boat tours.

  • Getting here

    Rijeka Airport is on Krk, about 20 km away, with car rental. By car over the Krk bridge: Munich 550 km, Vienna 530 km, Milan 540 km, Budapest 520 km. Buses from Rijeka run about every hour.
