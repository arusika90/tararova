02Your captain

Meet Captain Bobo

Bobo is a barkariol, a taxi boat driver, which is an old trade in Punat. Once there were more than 30 barkariols here. Today there are five.

He runs Taxi Boat Punat as a small family business with his wife Helena and their children. Guests know him as friendly, funny and full of stories about the island.

His boat IVEX is a traditional wooden fishing boat built in 1973: 9 m long, for up to 16 passengers, with a Perkins engine of 87 PS.

You may have seen the tours on GetMyBoat or Expedia. Here you book direct: one WhatsApp message to the family.

04On board

What every trip has

  1. 01Up to 15 guests

    Shared trips take at most 15 people, so book early. From 7 people, a private boat is often the better choice.

  2. 02Swim stops

    Clear Adriatic water in bays you can only reach by boat. Snorkel, swim into caves and through tunnels under the rocks. Bring your swimwear.

  3. 03Stories from the captain

    Bobo is friendly, funny and full of stories about Krk, the bays and the islands around Punat.

  4. 04IVEX, built in 1973

    A traditional wooden fishing boat, 9 m long, for up to 16 passengers. On the full day trip there is a small grill on board.

05Good to know

Before you step on board

Bobo at the info stand
Meeting point
The red and white info stand on the seafront (Obala), opposite the post office, 51521 Punat. The boats are right there.
Parking
Park by Hotel Falkensteiner, 250 to 300 m away, and walk along the sea. No coins? Pay at the TISAK kiosk with a photo of your number plate.
What to bring
Swimwear and a towel.
Payment
A deposit when you book. The rest on the day, to the captain: cash, card, PayPal, Revolut or Wise.
Weather and cancelling
Free cancellation up to 24 hours before. Bad weather or a problem on our side: your booking is refunded automatically.
Kids
Kids are welcome. Children up to 12 pay less on shared trips and the taxi boat.
Season
Shared trips 5 June to 20 September. Private trips 5 April to 31 October. Taxi boat all year.

06What's nearby

Around Punat bay

Punat sits on a sheltered bay on the island of Krk. Travel times by taxi boat from Punat.

  • KošljunThe monastery island among cypress trees, right in Punat bay. 8 minutes by boat.
  • Punat marina10 minutes by taxi boat, 7 € per person one way.
  • Krk townOne of the oldest towns in Croatia. 30 minutes by taxi boat.
  • PrnibaThe little port of St. Mikul. 8 minutes, 6 € per person one way.
  • Stara BaškaAbout 1 hour by boat, a lunch stop on the full day trip.
  • RabSandy beaches, about 2.5 hours by boat.
