02Your captain

Meet Captain Bobo

Bobo runs Taxi Boat Punat from the waterfront in Punat, on the island of Krk. Guests know him as friendly, funny and full of stories.

He takes small groups out to the wild bays, across to the island of Plavnik and over to the monastery on Košljun. Every trip comes with swim stops and the captain's own stories of the island.

You may have seen his tours on GetMyBoat or Expedia. Here you book direct: one WhatsApp message, straight to the captain.

06What's nearby

Around Punat bay

Punat sits on a sheltered bay on the island of Krk. A few places worth knowing.

  • KošljunAn island monastery among cypress trees, right in Punat bay. The taxi boat takes you across.
  • Punat marinaThe marina of Punat, on the same bay as the waterfront.
