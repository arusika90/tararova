About

Hi, I’m Maria.
I started with Flash.
Things escalated.

I started designing at 17. First animated Flash banners, then graphic design, exhibitions and events, and eventually digital products. I have a formal art education and graduated with honors.

I already knew how to interview people, find problems, structure a product, define scope, design the experience and test whether it made sense. Then I stopped stopping at Figma: now I build the software and ship it too.

So now I build my own.
