01The family

Generations of truffle hunters

Truffle hunting runs in the Prodan family. It has been passed down from one generation to the next, together with the woods and the dogs.

Today you are welcomed like a guest at home. Višnja explains what truffles are and how they grow. Her brother takes you into the woods with their trained dogs, Sky among them. Back at the house, Višnja cooks with the truffles while they are still fresh.

Buzet is called the city of truffles. Spend a morning here and you will see why.

Even Gordon Ramsay came truffle hunting with us. He brought luck: we found two truffles and cooked a meal together.

03Your day

From the woods to the table

Four simple steps. All you need to bring is curiosity and good shoes.

Buzet, Istria Praščari 43, Sv. Ivan
  2. Step 1

    Welcome

    We welcome you at the family home and explain what truffles are, black and white, and how the dogs find them.

  3. Step 2

    The hunt with the dogs

    Into the woods with Višnja's brother and the trained dogs, Sky among them. The dogs do the work, and you usually find truffles.

  4. Step 3

    Cooking

    Back in the kitchen, Višnja cooks with fresh truffles. If you like, she teaches you to make gnocchi.

  5. Step 4

    At the table

    A three-course truffle meal with homemade juice and local wine.
