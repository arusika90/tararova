02Opatija Boats

Small harbour, big bay

We leave from Zert, the small harbour in the middle of Opatija. From there the whole Kvarner opens up: the Lungomare from the water, Volosko's stone houses, Lovran, and the quiet coves and caves in between.

Choose a private tour with one of our skippers, or rent a boat and drive it yourself. We give you a proper briefing at pick-up, and we are one WhatsApp message away the whole time.

Everything is arranged over WhatsApp, quick and clear. Tell us the day and how many of you are coming, and we take it from there.

06Along the coast

What you see from the water

The Opatija riviera is best seen from the sea. These are the places our tours pass along the Kvarner coast.

  • Opatija LungomareThe famous seaside promenade, villas and all, seen the way it was meant to be seenRight by Zert
  • VoloskoA small fishing village with stone houses and a tiny harbour, a short hop northNorth
  • LovranOld town, chestnut woods above and coves below, south along the rivieraSouth

Hidden caves, snorkeling spots, cliff jumps and beaches are chosen on the day by your skipper, depending on sea and wind.
