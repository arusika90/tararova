02Opatija Boats

Small harbour, big bay

We leave from Zert, the port in the middle of Opatija, right next to Restaurant Molo. From there the whole Kvarner opens up: the Lungomare from the water, the Brseč cliffs, Mošćenička Draga and the islands of Cres and Krk.

Join a shared tour in a small group, book a private tour with one of our skippers, or rent a speedboat and drive it yourself. You will also find us in Rijeka, at Riva 10.

Everything is arranged over WhatsApp, quick and clear. Tell us the day and how many of you are coming, and we take it from there.

08Along the coast

What you see from the water

The Opatija Riviera is best seen from the sea. These are the places our tours visit along the Kvarner coast and on the islands.

  • Opatija LungomareThe famous seaside promenade, villas and all, seen the way it was meant to be seenRight by Zert
  • VoloskoA small fishing village with stone houses. Sunset Tour guests can board at the pier by the green lanternNorth
  • LovranOld town and quiet coves. Natura Pura also leaves from Lovran harbour, near Restaurant KvarnerSouth
  • BrsečHigh cliffs and clear water for swimming and snorkeling on Myths of the Sea and Natura PuraSouth
  • Mošćenička DragaA seaside village with a long beach, the stop on the Riviera Escape tourSouth
  • Cres · BeliSecluded beaches you can only reach by boat, and the old village of Beli on Wild CresIsland
  • Krk · NjiviceThree swimming spots on Krk and the village of Njivice on Krk AquaticaIsland
  • RijekaThe city in an hour by boat, and our second base at Riva 10East

Swim stops and beaches are chosen on the day by your skipper, depending on sea and wind. Some beaches are reached by a short swim from the boat.
