02Rent a Boat Punat

The oldest boat rental on Krk

We are the oldest boat rental company on the island of Krk, family-run, with 17 boats in the centre of Punat harbour. Many of our guests come back every summer.

From boats for 5 up to the Beneteau Flyer 7.7 for 10 people. Every boat has complete safety gear on board, and before you leave we show you everything right at the boat.

Booking is easy: one WhatsApp message with the day and how many of you, and we take it from there. Fair prices, friendly owners and staff.

04How it works

Four steps to the water

Punat harbour
  1. 1

    Book

    Pick a boat, a date and how many of you, and send it via WhatsApp. We confirm by message.

  2. 2

    Briefing at the boat

    Park nearby and come to the pier in the centre of Punat harbour. Check-in is at 09:00. We show you the boat and everything you need before you leave.

  3. 3

    Enjoy

    Head out to the beaches and bays of Krk and the nearby islands. Swim, explore, take your time.

  4. 4

    Return

    Bring the boat back by 19:00 with a full tank. That's it.
