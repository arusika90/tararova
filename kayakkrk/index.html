02Your guide

Meet Bernard

Bernard is a local guide here on Krk. He speaks fluent German and English, keeps the day flexible and is genuinely helpful, whether it is your first time in a kayak or you came for the big jump.

He shows you how to paddle, takes you to the snorkeling spots and out to the cliffs. Jump if you want to, cheer from the kayak if you don't.

04Your tour

How your day goes

Medane beach
  1. 1

    Message Bernard

    Pick a tour, a day and how many of you are coming. The form below opens WhatsApp with everything filled in.

  2. 2

    Meet at Medane beach

    Šetalište Antuna Žica-Ulivina 15 in Punat. Bernard gets you set up with your kayak and equipment.

  3. 3

    Paddle out

    A few tips on the beach, then out onto the clear water. Beginners and kids are welcome.

  4. 4

    Snorkel, jump, relax

    Snorkel over the pebbles, jump from the cliffs if you want to, then paddle back with a smile.

05Good to know

Before you paddle

A few practical things, so the day is easy. Anything else, just ask Bernard on WhatsApp.

Two cliffs, about 7 to 8 m and 13 to 15 m. Jumping is optional, watching is fun too.

Meeting point
Medane beach area, Šetalište Antuna Žica-Ulivina 15, 51521 Punat
Hours
Open daily 09:00 to 21:00 in season
What to bring
Swimwear, a towel, sunscreen, a hat and some water. Clothes you don't mind getting wet.
Cliff jumping
Always optional. Cliffs about 7 to 8 m and 13 to 15 m. Jump, or simply watch.
Kids
Welcome. The tours are easy for families with kids.
Weather
Kayaking depends on wind and sea. Bernard tells you on WhatsApp how the day looks.
Languages
Bernard speaks fluent German and English.
