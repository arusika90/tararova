01Welcome

A family house, 10 minutes from the beach

Villa Valis is a family project of the Čubranić family, built after many years of work in tourism. Comfort, quality and room to rest come first.

10 apartments for 2 to 4 guests, most of them with a view of the Adriatic. Every apartment has an extra bed for up to 2 children, and everyone shares the saltwater pool with hydromassage jets and waterfalls, plus a smaller pool made just for kids.

On arrival you are welcomed with a glass of homemade island drink and freshly picked figs. Ask us about boat trips, bike routes, walks, food and wine: we are happy to share our favourite places.

Write to us directly and you get the direct price and a real person on the other side.

04What's nearby

Baška, and the rest of Krk

  • Vela plaža

    Less than 10 minutes on foot. A long pebble beach where the sea gets deeper gradually, good for children, with a promenade for the evening.

  • Baška old town

    Narrow stone streets, small konobas and the harbour.

  • Out and about

    Diving, sailing, boat trips, paragliding, zip line, hiking and mountain biking, plus Baška's Glagolitic alphabet trail.

  • Island flavours

    A glass of homemade island drink and fresh figs when you arrive, and Krk lamb and island wine in the konobas below. Natalia knows where.

  • Krk town and Plitvice

    Krk town with its walls and cathedral is a lovely day trip. Further afield, the Plitvice Lakes, a UNESCO World Heritage Site.
