01The team and the bay

A small team, a big bay

Robert and the crew run Marine Sport from the harbour of Mošćenička Draga, on the Opatija riviera.

Boat dives with Goran at the helm, shore dives straight from the beach, and more than 16 dive sites across Kvarner bay. Igor, David, Dario and Robi guide mixed groups of all ages and levels, with good equipment and a calm, safety-first way of doing things.

Great visibility and usually warm water. Colourful nudibranchs and yellow sponges on the shore dives, and wreck dives including Lina and Vis for certified divers.

04Your dive day

How a dive day goes

Aleja Slatina 2
  1. 1

    Meet at the center

    Aleja Slatina 2, right by the bay. Paperwork, certification card if you have one, and a chat about the plan for the day.

  2. 2

    Gear up and briefing

    Your own equipment or rental from the center, checked together. The site, the depth and the signals are explained before anyone gets wet.

  3. 3

    Boat out or walk in

    Goran takes the RIB out to the site, or the group walks in from the shore for a reef dive.

  4. 4

    Dive, surface, log it

    Clear water, usually warm. Back at the center you fill in the logbook and plan the next one.
