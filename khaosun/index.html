01Our team

Hands trained in Thailand, a room in Opatija

Thai Touch is a team of therapists from Thailand. Each is a graduate of a leading school of traditional Thai medicine, with 5 to 10 years of experience.

Thai massage is a therapeutic art. Our therapists work with their hands, palms, arms, elbows, knees and feet, and sometimes with warm herbal compresses: pressing and rolling, squeezing, assisted stretching and gentle bending.

During the session the therapist checks how you feel and adjusts the pressure. Nothing is rushed, nothing is forced.

Our promise: only authentic treatments, fixed published prices and the same standard in all four Thai Touch salons, in Opatija, Rijeka, Mali Lošinj and Rab.
